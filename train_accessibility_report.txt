ISSUE-001 [ISSUE] Visible label | #from
Criterion / guidance: 3.3.2 — Labels or Instructions
Actual: The field has no visible purpose label. Computed accessible name: 'From station'.
Expected: A persistent visible label identifies the field.
Recommendation:
1. Provide a visible <label> for this field, e.g. "From station".
2. Use the unique control id "from" and set the label for attribute to "from".
3. Keep the label visible before and after entering a value.

ISSUE-029 [REVIEW] Terms panel design | #terms-dialog
Criterion / guidance: Manual design review — disclosure versus dialog
Actual: The revealed terms content is an ordinary div. Its name suggests a dialog, but the page does not establish modal behavior.
Expected: Choose and implement a consistent disclosure or dialog pattern.
Recommendation:
1. For an inline disclosure, use a button with aria-expanded and aria-controls; keep content in logical reading order.
